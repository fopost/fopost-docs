## Welcome to FoPost

**FoPost** publishes, schedules, and manages content across 30 social networks, from one place.

Use the dashboard if you are a creator or a team. Use the API if you are building something.

## For creators and teams

No code. The [dashboard](/guide/using-fopost/dashboard) does all of it.

- **Publish everywhere at once.** Write once, with a native version per network and a live preview of each
- **Schedule ahead.** A calendar you can drag, bulk import from a spreadsheet, and best times worked out from your own data
- **The [FoPost Agent](/guide/using-fopost/agent).** Ask for a campaign in plain language, review the drafts, approve what ships
- **Automate the repetitive parts.** Feeds, webhooks, and recycling your evergreen winners
- **Measure it.** Engagement, followers, top posts, and campaign roll-ups by label
- **Work as a team.** Workspaces with real isolation, roles, and approvals

## For developers

**The [Cloud API](/api/overview)** is the product behind the dashboard. One REST API for every network, with scheduling, media, analytics, automations, and webhooks. Official clients for [TypeScript](/sdks/typescript), [Python](/sdks/python), [PHP](/sdks/php), [Laravel](/sdks/laravel), and [MCP](/mcp).
