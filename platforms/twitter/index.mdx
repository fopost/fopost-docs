X gets the full surface: posts and thread chains, articles, media, per-post and account analytics, competitor tracking, and mentions, replies, and DMs in the inbox.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → X**, approve on X. The grant covers reading and writing posts, media upload, and direct messages, and tokens refresh automatically. Disconnecting revokes FoPost's authorisation on X's side too.

## At a glance

| Capability | Status |
|:-----------|:-------|
| Posts and thread chains | [Supported](/platforms/twitter/publishing) |
| Articles | [Supported, X Premium required](/platforms/twitter/publishing#articles) |
| Media | 4 photos, or 1 GIF, or 1 video per post |
| Analytics | [Post, account, and competitor metrics](/platforms/twitter/analytics) |
| Inbox | Mentions, replies, and DMs, with replies from FoPost |
| Deletion | Deleting in FoPost deletes on X |

## Limits that matter

FoPost accepts up to 25,000 characters, X's ceiling for Premium subscribers; a non-Premium account is held to X's standard 280 and X rejects anything longer at publish time. Every link costs a flat 23 characters, whatever its real length — the composer counts it that way too.
