## Posts

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Length | 25,000 characters with X Premium; 280 without |
| Links | Always count as 23 characters |
| Media | 4 photos, **or** 1 GIF, **or** 1 video |
| Image size | 5 MB (JPEG, PNG, WebP) |
| Video | MP4, up to 512 MB and 140 seconds |
| Alt text | Up to 1,000 characters per image |

Hashtags are appended after the text, then the post's link. Videos, GIFs, and large images upload in chunks and FoPost waits for X to finish processing before publishing. A file X rejects fails with X's reason.

## Threads

Blocks written as a thread in the composer publish as a reply chain. On the first post of a thread you can also set **who can reply** (everyone, accounts you follow, or only mentioned accounts) and publish into a **community** instead of the main feed.

## Articles

A post composed as an article publishes as a native X Article: a two-step draft-and-publish on X's side, with the companion post carrying it into the feed.

- **X Premium is required**: without it, X rejects the article and the post fails with that reason
- A **title** is required
- Headings (three levels), bold, italic, strikethrough, links, lists, and quotes carry over; images are placed where you put them; pasting the URL of an X post on its own line embeds that post
- Video and GIFs cannot go inside an article

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost deletes it on X.
