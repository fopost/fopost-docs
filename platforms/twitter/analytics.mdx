## Per post

Impressions, likes, replies, reposts, and quotes. Engagement is the sum of likes, replies, and reposts.

## Per account

Followers, following, and total posts, collected on a schedule so the [analytics](/guide/analytics) trends have history to draw.

Recent posts from your timeline are synced too, including posts published natively on X, so the top-posts ranking reflects the account, not just what went through FoPost. Reposts are excluded.

## Competitor tracking

Track any public X handle: their recent posts and public engagement numbers appear next to yours. X bills reads on its side, so heavy competitor polling can hit X's limits. FoPost backs off and retries rather than failing.

## Inbox

Mentions and replies land in the [inbox](/guide/inbox) on the background refresh. **DMs are fetched only when you refresh the inbox yourself**: X charges per message read, so the background poll leaves them alone. You can reply to all of them from FoPost, and a DM reply goes back into the same conversation.
