YouTube publishing uploads video to your channel — regular videos and Shorts — with thumbnails, privacy, and the usual upload settings handled per post.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → YouTube**, approve with Google and pick the channel — Brand Accounts are supported via Google's own account chooser. Tokens refresh automatically, and disconnecting revokes the grant on Google's side.

## At a glance

| Capability | Status |
|:-----------|:-------|
| Video uploads and Shorts | [Supported](/platforms/youtube/publishing) |
| Custom thumbnails | Supported, on verified channels |
| Analytics | [Video, channel, and viewer demographics](/platforms/youtube/analytics) |
| Competitor tracking | Supported — any public channel |
| Deletion | Deleting in FoPost deletes the video |
