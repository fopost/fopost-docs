title: YouTube Analytics
description: Video metrics, channel metrics, viewer demographics, and competitor channels.
---

## Per video

Views, likes, and comments.

## Per channel

Subscribers, video count, and lifetime channel views. Recent uploads are synced — including videos uploaded in YouTube Studio — so [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics) reflects the channel, not just what went through FoPost.

## Viewer demographics

Age, gender, and country of the viewers you reached over the last 90 days. YouTube reports these only above its own audience thresholds; below them the breakdown is empty rather than wrong.

## Competitor tracking

Track any public channel — by handle, channel URL, or channel ID — and see their recent videos, views, likes, and comments next to yours.
