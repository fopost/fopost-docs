FoPost publishes to a Snapchat **Public Profile** through Snap's Public Profile API. That is the profile a creator or business runs from Snapchat's Business Dashboard, not a personal Snapchat account.

## Connecting

**Connections → Snapchat**, then sign in with the Snapchat account that has a membership in the organization owning the profile. FoPost asks for the `snapchat-profile-api` scope and connects the first Public Profile it finds in your organizations.

One Public Profile per workspace slot. To publish to a second profile, connect it in another workspace.

## What you can publish

Snapchat is the most restrictive network FoPost supports: **exactly one media item per post, and never a text-only post.**

| Post type | What it is | Media | Text |
|:----------|:-----------|:------|:-----|
| Story | Expires after the window you pick | One video | None. Snapchat renders no caption |
| Saved Story | Stays on the profile permanently | One video | Title, up to 45 characters |
| Spotlight | Goes to the Spotlight feed | One video | Description, up to 160 characters, hashtags included |

Pick the type in the composer under the account's **Snapchat Settings**. Story is the default.

### Media limits

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Items per post | 1 |
| Format | MP4 |
| Duration, Story and Saved Story | 5 to 60 seconds |
| Duration, Spotlight | 6 to 60 seconds |
| File size | 1 GB |
| Minimum resolution | 540 x 960 |
| Recommended aspect ratio | 9:16 |

A post with no media, with two or more items, with a non-MP4 file, or with a video outside the duration window fails preflight and never leaves FoPost.

### Story lifetime

A Story defaults to 24 hours. Set **Story lifetime** in the composer to keep it up for two days, three days, or a week.

## Analytics

Story, Saved Story, and Spotlight metrics come back as views, unique viewers, screenshots, shares, and replies, plus view time. Profile-level numbers carry subscribers and subscribers gained. Snapchat serves at most the past 90 days.

## Deletion

Snapchat's Public Profile API exposes no delete for a published Story, Saved Story, or Spotlight. Deleting the post in FoPost removes the FoPost record; remove the Snap itself from the Snapchat Business Dashboard.
