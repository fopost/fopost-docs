---
title: Kick
description: Chat messages on your own Kick channel.
---

Kick has no feed — a Kick "post" is a message in your own channel's chat.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Kick**, approve on Kick. Tokens refresh automatically.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Message length | 500 characters — longer text is cut off |
| Media, links, titles, tags | Not supported — chat is text |

The post body is sent to your channel's chat as you.

## Analytics and deletion

Chat is ephemeral: no analytics, and no deletion from FoPost.
