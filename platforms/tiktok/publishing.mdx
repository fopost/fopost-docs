## Post shapes

- **Video**: one MP4, MOV, or WebM
- **Photo post**: a carousel of up to 35 JPEG, PNG, or WebP images

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Caption | 2,200 characters, hashtags appended |
| Title | Up to 150 characters (from the post title, or the start of the caption) |
| Video size | Up to 4 GB |
| Video length | Up to 10 minutes, but your **creator account's own ceiling governs** (TikTok grants 3, 5, or 10 minutes per account), and FoPost reads it before publishing |
| Image size | 20 MB each |
| Links | Not supported in posts |

## Per-post settings

The settings TikTok requires a posting tool to expose:

- **Privacy**: who can view the post
- **Allow comments**, and for video **allow Duet** and **allow Stitch**
- **AI-generated content** label
- **Commercial content disclosure**: your brand, branded content, or both

## Creator limits

Several posting switches are set on the TikTok account itself, not in FoPost: whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, which privacy levels are open, and how long a video the account may post. The composer reads them and disables anything TikTok has turned off, rather than offering a toggle that will be refused.

```bash
curl https://api.fopost.com/v1/accounts/{account_id}/tiktok/creator-info \
  -H "X-API-Key: $FOPOST_API_KEY"
```

Change any of them in the TikTok app and the composer picks it up on the next load.

## Not available

TikTok's public API has no endpoint for these, so FoPost does not offer them:

- **Commercial Music Library search**, and no music field on a post to attach a track to
- **Location and place tagging**: no place search, and no place field on a post
- **Video download by URL**: nothing returns a video file, so a TikTok link cannot be repurposed from its media. Repurpose from a transcript or the page text instead

## TikTok's guardrails

TikTok enforces its own posting pace. Too many posts in a day, or too many still processing, and TikTok refuses with a clear reason; FoPost backs off and the post retries rather than dying. If a video's format is refused, TikTok wants MP4 or MOV with H.264, 3 seconds to 10 minutes.

## Deleting

TikTok's posting API cannot delete. Remove the post in the TikTok app; FoPost keeps its record either way.
