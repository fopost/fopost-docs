title: TikTok

---

TikTok publishing posts directly to your account — video or photo carousels — with TikTok's own disclosure and interaction settings handled per post.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → TikTok**, approve on TikTok. Tokens refresh automatically, and disconnecting revokes the grant on TikTok's side.

## At a glance

| Capability | Status |
|:-----------|:-------|
| Video posts and photo carousels | [Supported](/platforms/tiktok/publishing) |
| Video length | Your creator account's own ceiling — TikTok grants 3, 5, or 10 minutes, and FoPost checks before publishing |
| Analytics | [Video and account metrics](/platforms/tiktok/analytics) |
| Deletion | Not available — TikTok's posting API cannot delete; remove posts in the app |
