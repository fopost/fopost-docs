## Per video

Views, likes, comments, and shares.

## Per account

Followers, following, total likes, and video count. Recent videos are synced (including ones posted in the TikTok app), so [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics) reflects the account, not just what went through FoPost.

## Not available

Audience demographics and competitor tracking are not available for TikTok.
