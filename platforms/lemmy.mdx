title: Lemmy
description: Posts to a community on any Lemmy instance.
---

Lemmy is federated: you connect to your own instance and post into one community per connection.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Lemmy**, enter your instance address, username, password, and the community — `technology`, or `technology@lemmy.ml` for a community on another instance. Accounts with two-factor authentication enabled cannot connect today.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Body | 10,000 characters |
| Title | 200 characters — from the post title, or the first line of the body |
| Link | One URL — the post's link |
| Image | One, used as the post's link |

A Lemmy post is a title plus an optional body and one optional link; an attached image becomes that link. There is no native media upload, no tags, and no thread chains.

## Analytics

Per post: upvotes and comments. Recent posts are included in [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics); account-level Lemmy reports a combined post-and-comment count rather than followers. Competitor tracking works — track any public Lemmy user your instance can see.

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost deletes it on the instance.
