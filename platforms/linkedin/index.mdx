LinkedIn publishing covers personal profiles and company pages, with the widest media palette of any network here: images, video, and PDF documents in one post shape.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → LinkedIn**, approve on LinkedIn. A personal profile connects directly; posting as a company page requires admin on that page. Tokens refresh automatically, and disconnecting revokes the grant on LinkedIn's side.

## At a glance

| Capability | Status |
|:-----------|:-------|
| Text, image, multi-image, video, document, and link posts | [Supported](/platforms/linkedin/publishing) |
| Image carousels | Supported — delivered as a document, the shape LinkedIn swipes |
| Native articles | Not possible — LinkedIn has no article API; an article publishes as its link post |
| Analytics | [Post and page metrics](/platforms/linkedin/analytics), richer for company pages |
| Demographics | Company pages only, by country |
| Deletion | Deleting in FoPost deletes on LinkedIn |
