title: LinkedIn Analytics
description: What LinkedIn reports for profiles, and the extra metrics company pages get.
---

What LinkedIn gives back depends on what kind of account is connected — and the gaps below are LinkedIn's API surface, not FoPost holding data back.

## Personal profiles

Per post: likes and comments. LinkedIn does not expose follower counts or impressions for personal profiles through its API, so profile-level growth charts have nothing to draw from.

## Company pages

Everything above, plus per-post impressions, clicks, and shares, and page follower counts — the full [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics) experience. Recent page posts are synced, including ones published on LinkedIn directly.

**Follower demographics** are available for company pages, broken down by country.

## Not available

Competitor tracking is not available for LinkedIn.
