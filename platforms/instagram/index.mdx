Instagram is one network with two ways to connect, and both publish the same things.

## Connecting

A **Business or Creator** account is required either way: Instagram's API does not publish to personal accounts.

- **Directly**: **Accounts → Connect → Instagram** signs in with Instagram itself
- **Through Facebook**: connect via the Facebook login and pick the Instagram account linked to one of your Pages. This is the route to take if your Instagram is managed alongside a Facebook Page, and it is the one that supports [competitor tracking](/platforms/instagram/analytics#competitor-tracking)

Tokens are long-lived and refresh automatically.
