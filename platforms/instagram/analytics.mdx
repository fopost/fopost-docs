title: Instagram Analytics

---

## Per post

Views, reach, likes, comments, shares, and saves. Reels add watch time and average watch time. Metrics arrive per Instagram's insight rules for the post's type.

## Per account

Followers, following, post count, plus daily reach and profile views. Recent posts are synced — including ones published in the Instagram app — so [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics) reflects the account, not just what went through FoPost.

## Audience demographics

Country, city, age, and gender, for three audiences: your **followers**, the accounts you **engaged**, and the accounts you **reached** in the last 30 days. Instagram reports demographics only above its own minimum audience size; below it the breakdown is empty rather than wrong.

## Competitor tracking

Track any **public Business or Creator** Instagram account — recent posts, likes, and comments next to yours. This runs through Instagram's business-discovery lookup, so it is available on connections made **through Facebook**; a directly-connected account can be tracked as a competitor by others but cannot itself track.
