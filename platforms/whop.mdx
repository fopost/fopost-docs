Whop publishing creates forum posts in a community you manage.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Whop**, approve on Whop, then pick which of your companies the connection posts to (one connection per company). By default posts land in the community's public forum. Tokens refresh automatically.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Body | 10,000 characters |
| Title | Optional |
| Link | Appended to the body |
| Media and tags | Not supported |

## Analytics and deletion

Whop's API exposes neither metrics nor forum-post deletion, so there are no analytics and no deletion from FoPost. Manage published posts in Whop itself.
