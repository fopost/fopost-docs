---
title: Twitch
description: Chat messages and announcements on your own channel.
---

Twitch has no feed to post to — a Twitch "post" is a message in your own channel's chat, or a highlighted announcement.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Twitch**, approve on Twitch. Tokens refresh automatically.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Message length | 500 characters — longer text is cut off |
| Media | Not supported — chat is text |

The post body plus its link, sent as either:

- a **chat message** — a normal message from your account
- an **announcement** — Twitch's highlighted banner message, with a colour option (blue, green, orange, purple, or your channel's primary)

Twitch can accept a message and still drop it — channel chat restrictions like follower-only mode apply — and when that happens the post fails with Twitch's reason.

## Analytics and deletion

Chat is ephemeral: no analytics, and no deletion from FoPost.
