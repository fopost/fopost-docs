WhatsApp is in preparation. The pages and the API below are built, and the connection opens once the platform review of the messaging permissions clears.

You bring the number. FoPost connects to a WhatsApp Business Account you already own and control; it does not sell phone numbers, and SMS and voice calling are outside what it does.

## Connecting

Two ways, and they suit different customers.

- **Guided signup** (recommended): in **Connections → WhatsApp**, choose **Connect WhatsApp**. The platform's own onboarding runs, you pick or add a business number, and FoPost then asks which number on the account to connect. One number per workspace.
- **Your own credentials**: if you already run the number yourself, connect with the WhatsApp Business Account id, the phone number id, and a system user token. Useful when the number is managed outside FoPost.

Either way the token is stored encrypted and never leaves the server.

## The 24 hour messaging window

This is the rule that shapes everything else, so it is worth reading once.

After a customer messages you, you may reply freely for **24 hours**. Once that window shuts, WhatsApp accepts only an **approved template message** from you. Sending a free-form message after the window is refused by the platform, and refusals are what lower a number's quality rating.

FoPost enforces the window before it sends. A free-form reply outside it is refused here with `window_closed`, and nothing reaches the platform. A template message is allowed either side of the window, which is the reason templates exist.

## Inbox

Inbound messages land in the [inbox](/guide/inbox), threaded by person: one number, one continuous conversation with their whole history.

- **Text, media, and documents**, sent and received in the thread
- **Taps on buttons and list rows**, recorded as the answer the customer gave
- **Flow submissions**, recorded on the message they arrived as
- **Media by reference**: an attachment is stored as the platform's media id, and the file is fetched server-side when someone opens it, so no expiring link is ever handed out

Replies go out from the inbox like any other network, subject to the window above.

## Template messages

A template is how a conversation starts and how you reach someone after the window shuts. Templates live on the account, not in FoPost: the platform reviews each one and owns its status.

Open the account in **Connections** and choose **Open Templates** to write one, submit it, and read the status the platform gave it. You can also adapt one of the platform's pre-written library templates instead of drafting from nothing.

Over the API:

| Endpoint | What it does |
|:---------|:-------------|
| `GET /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/templates` | Every template with its review status |
| `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/templates` | Files a template for review |
| `GET /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/templates/library` | The platform's pre-written templates |
| `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/templates/import` | Creates one from a library entry |
| `PATCH /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/templates/{templateId}` | Edits the components or category |
| `DELETE /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/templates/{templateId}` | Removes it from the account |

A newly submitted template comes back as **In Review**. Nothing marks it approved until the platform does.

## Flows

A flow is a short form the customer fills in inside the chat: a booking, a sign-up, a support triage. The builder in FoPost is a form, not a canvas, because a flow is a list of questions.

Open the account and choose **Open Flows** to name one, pick its category, and add the questions. The flow is created as a draft and published when you are ready; a published flow is deprecated rather than deleted.

| Endpoint | What it does |
|:---------|:-------------|
| `GET /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows` | Flows on the account, with validation errors |
| `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows` | Creates a draft |
| `PUT /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows/{flowId}/json` | Replaces the screens |
| `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows/{flowId}/publish` | Makes it sendable |
| `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows/{flowId}/deprecate` | Retires a published flow |
| `GET /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows/responses` | What people submitted |

A flow that reads live data calls an endpoint of yours, and the platform encrypts those payloads. Register the public half of your key with `PUT /v1/accounts/{id}/whatsapp/flows/encryption-key`; the private half stays with you and is never sent to FoPost.

## Profile, groups, blocking, and commerce

| Surface | What it covers |
|:--------|:---------------|
| **Profile** | About text, address, description, websites, vertical, and the profile picture. The display name is a review request, not a write: the number keeps the old name until it passes |
| **Groups** | Create a group on the number, read its info, update it, and get or reset the invite link. Participation is invite-only: there is no endpoint that adds someone, so you send the link |
| **Blocking** | Block and unblock numbers, and list the blocked ones. Up to 100 per call |
| **Commerce** | Turn the cart and the catalog on or off, and point the number at a catalog you already own |
| **Account state** | The account review and verification state, plus the number's quality rating, name status, and messaging limit tier |

## Broadcasts

A template message can go to many people at once through a broadcast, to contacts who already have a conversation with you. Free-form bulk messaging is not something the platform allows.

## What the platform charges

WhatsApp bills **your own** WhatsApp Business Account for the conversations it charges for. FoPost does not resell or mark up that cost. What the platform reported for a message, including its billing category, is kept as a note beside it so you can read your own platform cost.

## Click to WhatsApp ads

When someone taps a Click to WhatsApp ad, the ad that earned the conversation rides on their first message. FoPost keeps that referral, so a conversation can be attributed to the campaign that produced it.

## Sandbox

Before your own number clears review, you can see a WhatsApp thread work end to end. `POST /v1/whatsapp/sandbox/sessions` invites one tester: a platform-owned test number sends them an approved template, and their reply opens the window and activates the session. The tester's number is not stored, only a keyed digest of it and the last four digits.

## What it will not do

- **Buy a phone number.** Bring your own WhatsApp Business number
- **Send SMS or place calls.** Out of scope
- **Publish a post.** WhatsApp delivers messages to people, not posts to a feed. There is nothing to schedule into a feed here
- **Bulk message strangers.** Templates reach people who already have a conversation with you
