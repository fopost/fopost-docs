Listmonk is a self-hosted newsletter server, and the FoPost connection turns a post into a campaign sent to one of its lists.

<Callout type="warn">
Publishing to Listmonk **sends email immediately** to everyone on the target list. There is no review step on the Listmonk side; the review is your FoPost schedule.
</Callout>

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Listmonk**, enter your instance address, an API user and its token, and the numeric ID of the list to send to. An optional sender override replaces the instance's default sender.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Body | 200,000 characters, rendered to HTML email |
| Subject | From the post title, or the first line of the body |
| Media | Not uploaded; images must be URLs in the body |
| Link and tags | Not supported as separate fields |

Publishing creates the campaign and starts it in one motion. The post links to the campaign in your Listmonk admin.

## Analytics

Per campaign: views and clicks, as Listmonk tracks them. Account-level: the target list's subscriber count.

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost deletes the campaign record from Listmonk. Email already delivered stays delivered; nothing can unsend it.
