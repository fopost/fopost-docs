Facebook publishing targets **Pages**. Personal profiles cannot be published to. That is Facebook's rule, not FoPost's.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Facebook**, approve, and pick the Page. You need a role on the Page that allows publishing. Each Page you manage connects as its own account, and tokens refresh automatically.

## At a glance

| Capability | Status |
|:-----------|:-------|
| Text, link, photo, multi-photo, and video posts | [Supported](/platforms/facebook/publishing) |
| Page insights | [Impressions, reach, reactions, video metrics](/platforms/facebook/analytics) |
| Audience demographics | Not available |
| Deletion | Deleting in FoPost deletes on the Page |
