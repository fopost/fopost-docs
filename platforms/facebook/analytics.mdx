---
title: Facebook Analytics
description: Post insights and Page-level metrics.
---

## Per post

Impressions, reach, clicks, comments, shares, and reactions — with the reaction breakdown (like, love, and the rest) kept separate. Video posts add views, total watch time, and average watch time.

## Per Page

Followers, published post count, 28-day reach and page views, and follower adds and removes over the period — enough for [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics) to draw growth honestly, including the losses.

Recent Page posts are synced too, including ones published directly on Facebook, so top-posts rankings reflect the Page rather than the tool.

## Not available

Audience demographics and competitor tracking are not available for Facebook.
