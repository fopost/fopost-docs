Google Business Profile posts appear on your business listing in Search and Maps.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Google Business Profile**, approve with the Google account that manages the business, then pick the location. One connection per location, so a multi-location business connects each one it posts to.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Text | 1,500 characters |
| Image | One JPEG or PNG, between 10 KB and 5 MB, at least 400×300 |
| Video | Not supported; a video attachment is blocked before publishing |
| Tags | Not supported |

### Post types

Pick the type in the composer's Google Business section.

| Type | What it is | What it needs |
|:-----|:-----------|:--------------|
| Update | A plain post with an optional photo and button | Nothing beyond the text |
| Event | A dated happening shown with its schedule | A title and a start and end time |
| Offer | A promotion with a coupon and redemption terms | A title and a schedule; the coupon, link and terms are optional |

### Buttons

An update or event carries one call-to-action: **Book**, **Order Online**, **Shop**, **Learn More**, **Sign Up** or **Call Now**, or no button at all. Every one but Call Now takes a link; Call Now uses the phone number on the listing. An offer renders its own redemption button instead, so it takes no separate call-to-action.

Through the API these arrive as per-account platform settings: `post_type`, `cta_action`, `cta_url`, `event_title`, `event_start`, `event_end`, `offer_coupon_code`, `offer_redeem_url` and `offer_terms`.

## Reviews

Reviews left on the location arrive in the inbox under **Reviews**, each with its star rating and the reviewer's name. Answering one posts the response on Google as the business; Google allows a single response per review, so sending again replaces the previous one.

Facebook Page recommendations land in the same place, under the same type. A recommendation has no stars of its own, so a positive one reads as five and a negative one as one.

Reviews are read through the Business Profile API, which Google grants per project. Until that grant is in place the inbox lists Google Business under **Soon** and the reply endpoint answers `503`.

## Analytics

None; post metrics are not read back. Check performance in your Business Profile dashboard.

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost removes it from the listing.
