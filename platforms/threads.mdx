Threads is connected on its own — it is a separate authorisation from Facebook and Instagram, on Threads' own screen.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Threads**, approve on Threads' authorisation screen. The grant covers publishing and reading insights. Tokens are long-lived and FoPost refreshes them in the background.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Post length | 500 characters |
| Media per post | Up to 20 images or videos in a carousel |
| Image size | 8 MB (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) |
| Video | MP4, up to 100 MB and 5 minutes |
| Alt text | Supported per item |

A post is plain text, a single image or video, or a carousel of up to 20 items. Links go into the text; hashtags are appended after the link. Thread chains are supported: blocks written as a thread in the composer publish as replies to each other.

A carousel is all-or-nothing — if one item fails on Threads' side, the whole post fails rather than going out incomplete.

## Analytics

Per post: views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, and shares. Per account: follower count and views. Posts published natively in the Threads app are included in [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics), not only ones sent through FoPost.

Follower demographics — country, city, age, and gender — are reported once your account clears Threads' own minimum audience size. Below it, Threads declines to say, and the breakdown is empty rather than wrong.

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost deletes it on Threads.
