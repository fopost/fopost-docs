Shopify publishing writes an **article** to a blog on your store. Unlike most networks, the connection is two-way: FoPost can also list, read, change and delete the articles already on the store, and read and update the store's products.

## Connecting

Two ways in, and both reach the same blogs and products:

- **Install the app**: **Accounts → Connect → Shopify**, enter your store domain (`your-store.myshopify.com`), and approve the permissions on Shopify. The token does not expire.
- **Admin API access token**: create a custom app in **Settings → Apps and sales channels → Develop apps** inside your own store admin, grant it the four permissions below, install it, and paste its Admin API access token into FoPost.

Either way the store needs these permissions: `read_content` and `write_content` for blogs and articles, `read_products` and `write_products` for the catalogue.

<Callout type="info">
  Only `*.myshopify.com` store domains are accepted. That is the host Shopify's Admin API answers on; your customer-facing custom domain will not work here.
</Callout>

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Body | 100,000 characters, rendered to HTML: headings, lists, quotes, code, and inline images all carry over |
| Title | From the post title, or the start of the body |
| Image | One featured image (JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP), 20 MB |
| Tags | Carried over to the article's tags |
| Link | Appended to the article body |

A post published to Shopify goes to the store's first blog unless you pick another one. Per post you can publish live or hold the article as a **draft** to finish in the store admin.

## Managing articles already on the store

The store's own articles are readable and writable, whether FoPost created them or not.

- **List** every article on a blog, drafts included, newest first
- **Read** one article in full
- **Create** a new article on any blog
- **Update** a live article in place. Only the fields you send change, and the article is addressed by its own id, so an edit never leaves a duplicate behind
- **Delete** an article from the store

Ask the agent for "what's on the store blog" to list them, and see [Blogs and articles](/api/blogs) for the API.

## Products

FoPost can list the store's products with their status, price, vendor, type and tags, and update a product's title, description, tags, type, vendor or status. Nothing in the catalogue changes unless you ask for it.

## Inbox

Not supported: Shopify blog comments have no API FoPost can read or answer.

## Analytics

None: the Admin API exposes no per-article metrics.

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost deletes the article on the store permanently. This cannot be undone.
