Skool has no official API, so FoPost publishes the way the Skool app itself does: it signs in with your credentials and creates the post in your community.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Skool**, enter your Skool email, password, and the community — its slug or URL.

Because this is a sign-in rather than an API grant, Skool occasionally challenges an automated login. When that happens the post fails with a clear message; retrying usually succeeds. Skool changing how its app works can also break publishing until FoPost catches up — that is the honest cost of a network with no API.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Body | 50,000 characters |
| Title | 200 characters — from the post title, or the first line of the body |
| Media, links, tags | Not supported |

## Analytics and deletion

No analytics — Skool exposes no metrics to read. Deleting the post in FoPost does delete it in the community.
