Tumblr publishing posts to your primary blog.

## Connecting

**Accounts → Connect → Tumblr**, approve on Tumblr. The connection targets your primary blog, and tokens refresh automatically.

## What you can publish

| Limit | Value |
|:------|:------|
| Body | 4,096 characters |
| Images | Up to 30 (JPEG, PNG, or GIF), 10 MB each |
| Video | Not supported today |
| Title and link | A post with a link renders it as a link block |
| Tags | Supported, as Tumblr tags |
| Alt text | Up to 4,096 characters per image |

A post is text plus optional images, in order, with an optional link block. Video appears in Tumblr's own apps but is not deliverable from FoPost today.

## Analytics

Tumblr rolls likes, reblogs, and replies into one **note count**, and that is what FoPost reads per post. Account-level: followers and post count. Posts made directly on Tumblr are included in [analytics](/guide/using-fopost/analytics). Competitor tracking works — track any public Tumblr blog.

## Deleting

Deleting the post in FoPost deletes it on Tumblr.
