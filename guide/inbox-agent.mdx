A drafted reply is only useful if it is true. Without anything to go on, AI writes a reply that sounds right and states a refund window nobody agreed to.

The knowledge base is what it reads first. You save your own answers, your own pages and your own files; a drafted reply quotes them instead of inventing something.

## What you can save

Add them under **Settings → Knowledge**. Each source is indexed in the background and shows **Ready** when it can be used.

## How a reply uses it

When you ask for a suggested reply in the [inbox](./inbox), FoPost looks up the passages closest to what the person actually asked and puts them in front of the model with an instruction not to go beyond them. A reply about your shipping times comes from your shipping page.

If nothing you saved answers the question, nothing is added. The reply is drafted without it rather than padded with a passage that nearly fits — a nearly-right answer is worse than a general one.

Nothing sends on its own. A drafted reply still waits for you, exactly as it did before.

## In an automation

The **AI** step in a [workflow](./automations) has a **knowledge** switch. Turned on, that step reads the knowledge base the same way a suggested reply does. It is off by default, so a workflow you already built keeps answering exactly as it did.

## Scoped to your workspace

A source belongs to one workspace and is never read from another. An agency answering for three clients cannot have one client's policy end up in another client's reply.

A source can also be narrowed to a single [brand](./brand-voices), for an account that runs more than one.

## Keeping it current

A `URL` source is a snapshot, not a live read. Edit the page and the old text keeps answering until you press **Sync Now**. Editing a saved FAQ or note re-indexes it automatically.

Delete a source and its passages go with it.

## Billing

Indexing a source and looking one up are both billed as AI usage from your prepaid balance. A sync is charged once per run, however long the source is, and nothing is charged if your balance is empty or the run fails.
