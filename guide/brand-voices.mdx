A brand in FoPost is the whole identity, not just a writing style: how you sound, and what you look like.

Included with every account.

## What a brand holds

**Voice.** Tone, vocabulary, things you say, things you never say, the reading level you aim at. This is what AI follows when it writes.

**Visuals.** Your colour palette and fonts, folded into anything generated for you.

**Assets.** Logos, product shots, people, and backgrounds, tagged by their role so a generator knows a logo is a logo.

## Why it exists

Ask a generic AI for a post and you get generic output. Give it your voice and it produces something you would have written, which is the difference between a draft you edit and a draft you delete.

The other half is consistency across people. Three teammates and an agent writing from the same brand sound like one company.

## Setting one up

**Brands → New**. Describe the tone in your own words, paste a few posts that sound right, list what to avoid. Add the palette and fonts, then upload the assets.

The examples do the most work. Two or three real posts beat a paragraph of adjectives.

## The default

One brand per workspace is the default and is used whenever you do not pick one. A single-brand workspace sets it once and never thinks about it again; an agency picks per post.

## Using it

Choose a brand when asking the [agent](./agent) or the composer for content. The voice shapes the writing, and the palette, fonts, and assets shape anything generated visually.

A request with no brand is untouched, so you can still ask for something off-brand deliberately.

## People assets

An asset tagged as a person needs a consent attestation before it can be saved. That is not paperwork for its own sake: generating video of a person who has not agreed to it is the kind of thing that ends up in a lawsuit, and the attestation is where you confirm you have permission.
