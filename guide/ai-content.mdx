AI is available in the composer as well as in the [agent](./agent), for the smaller jobs that do not need a conversation.

Every AI action is opt-in. Nothing rewrites your post unless you ask.

## In the composer

**Captions.** Write or improve a caption from what the post is about.

**Rewrite per network.** One draft becomes a native version for each target: shorter for X, structured for LinkedIn, visual-led for Instagram. Same message, right shape.

**Hashtags.** Suggestions that fit the network, since what reads as normal on Instagram reads as spam on LinkedIn.

**Editing actions.** Fix grammar, improve clarity, make it punchier, simplify, shorten, expand, rephrase, add emoji, change tone, translate. Applied to a selection or the whole post.

**Alt text.** Written for uploaded images automatically, and editable.

## Blog and URL to social

Paste a URL and get a post per network, written from the article rather than from its title. Good for a publishing schedule that trails a blog.

Uses AI credits from your prepaid balance.

## Voice to content

Record a voice note and get posts from it. Useful when the thought arrives while walking and would otherwise not survive to a keyboard.

Uses AI credits.

## Any language

AI writes in whatever language you do. Ask in Spanish, get Spanish, with the network conventions of that language rather than translated English.

## What it costs

AI usage is metered in dollars and paid from a prepaid balance as you use it, never billed after the fact. Add funds from the card on file, or let auto-reload keep the balance topped up; the first card you save starts it with $3. When the balance is empty, AI pauses and everything else keeps working. See [Billing](/billing).

What is left is in **Settings → AI Usage**, and in the account menu.

## What you never pick

You choose an outcome, not a model. For image and video there are three quality tiers, Draft, Standard, and Best; everywhere else there is just the feature. Which models sit behind them is our problem, and changing one is not something you should have to notice.
