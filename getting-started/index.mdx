Five steps from nothing to a live post. Nothing here needs a card or any code, and most of the time goes to one network's authorisation screen.

## 1. Sign up

Create your account at [fopost.com/dashboard](https://fopost.com/dashboard) — email, Google, or GitHub. Your first two connected accounts are free, no card required, so you can finish this whole page before deciding anything.

## 2. Connect one account

Go to **Accounts**, click **Connect** next to a network, approve on that network's own screen, and you are back. FoPost never sees or asks for your password.

Pick whichever network you post to most. A few have prerequisites on their own side — Instagram wants a Business or Creator account, for instance — and [Connecting Accounts](/guide/using-fopost/connecting-accounts) covers each one. The full list of networks is on [Supported Platforms](/platforms).

## 3. Write the post

Click **New Post**, type your text, and tick the account you just connected. The preview on the right renders the post as that network will actually show it, so a truncated line or a badly cropped image surfaces now instead of after it ships.

Attach an image if you like — it lands in your [Media Library](/guide/using-fopost/media-library) and stays reusable. [Creating Posts](/guide/using-fopost/creating-posts) covers per-network overrides, threads, and the rest of the composer.

## 4. Publish, or schedule it

Two buttons, one decision:

- **Publish** sends it now
- **Schedule** picks a date and time, and the post goes out then whether or not your browser is open

Scheduled posts live on the [calendar](/guide/using-fopost/calendar), where you can drag them around until they ship.

## 5. Confirm it landed

The post shows a per-account delivery status with a link to the live item. If a network rejected it, the reason is right on the post and one click retries just that account.

That is the whole loop: everything else in FoPost is this loop with more leverage.
