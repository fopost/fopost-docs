# Analytics

Numbers from every connected account in one place, collected in the background on a schedule.

## Cross-network leaderboard

Every account side by side: engagement, followers, posts, and the change over the period. The question analytics should answer first is "which of these is actually working", and a leaderboard answers it faster than five dashboards.

## Top posts

Ranked by engagement, over whatever range you pick. It includes posts published natively on the network, not only ones sent through FoPost, so the ranking reflects the account rather than the tool.

## Audience growth

Follower counts over time, per network. Networks vary in how often they report and how far back, so expect a stepped line on some rather than a smooth one.

## Who your audience is

Demographic breakdowns for the networks that report them, and only above their own minimum audience sizes. Gaps here are the network declining to say, not a collection failure.

## Best times

Worked out from your own engagement history per account, and offered when you schedule. It needs a few weeks of history before it means anything, and it is a suggestion rather than a rule.

## Posting streak

A year of posting activity, one square per day. Consistency is most of what compounds on social, and this is the view that makes an honest four-day gap visible.

## Campaign roll-up

[Labels](./labels) aggregate. Label posts by campaign or client and you get performance for the campaign rather than for individual posts spread across accounts.

Label at publish time. There is no way to reconstruct it later.

## Competitors

Track public accounts and see their numbers next to yours: posting frequency, engagement, growth. Useful for calibration, since "engagement is down" means something different if it is down across your whole category.

## Refresh and export

Metrics collect on a schedule. Collect now after publishing something you want to measure immediately; it is throttled, so it is not a substitute for waiting.

Export to CSV for anything you would rather do in a spreadsheet, or read the same figures through the [analytics API](/api/analytics).

## Basic and advanced

Starter gets engagement and follower counts. [Pro](/guide/plans/pro) and above add trends, cross-network comparison, demographics, best times, and the label roll-up.
