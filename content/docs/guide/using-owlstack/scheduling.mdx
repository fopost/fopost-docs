# Scheduling

Write now, publish later. Scheduled posts go out from our servers, so nothing depends on your browser being open.

## Schedule one

In the composer, click **Schedule** instead of Publish and pick a date and time. Times are in the workspace timezone, set in **Settings**, so a team spread across timezones agrees on what 9am means.

## Best times

Analytics works out when your audience actually engages, per account, from your own history rather than an industry average. Those slots are suggested when you pick a time. Ignore them freely: they are a starting point, not a rule, and they need a few weeks of history before they say anything useful.

## Repeat a post

A post can go out on a cadence without you scheduling each occurrence: set how many times and how far apart, in hours, days, weeks, or months. The post shows how many runs are left.

For resurfacing your best evergreen posts with a fresh variant each time rather than the same text repeatedly, use [recycling](./recycling) instead.

## Change your mind

Drag a post to a new slot on the [calendar](./calendar), or open it and change the time. To move many at once, select them and shift the whole set by an offset.

**Cancel** pulls a scheduled post out of the queue. Anything already published stays published.

## What happens at the time

The post is queued, then each account is delivered independently. A network that is slow or briefly down is retried automatically with a growing gap between attempts.

The post's status summarises it: **published** when every account landed, **partially failed** when some did not. Open it to see which, and why.

## When something fails

The reason is on the post in plain language. Retry sends only the accounts that failed.

Some failures are worth retrying and some are not. A rate limit or a network blip is transient. An expired token, content over the limit, or a rejected media type will fail identically every time, so fix the cause first. An expired token usually means [reconnecting the account](./connecting-accounts).

## Many at once

To schedule a month of content from a spreadsheet, see [Bulk Scheduling](./bulk-scheduling).
