## Everything in Pro, plus

- **Unlimited social accounts.** No counting, no per-account maths when you take on a client
- **Voice to content.** Record a voice note and get posts per network from it
- **Ten workspaces**, one per client, fully isolated from each other
- **Ten seats**, with per-workspace roles so a contractor sees one client and not the rest
- **Higher API rate limit**, sized for automating at agency volume

## Why workspaces matter here

A workspace is a hard boundary. Accounts, posts, media, analytics, and team membership all live inside one, and nothing crosses. An API key can be bound to a single workspace, so an integration built for one client physically cannot reach another.

That is the difference between "we are careful" and "it cannot happen".

## Growing past it

Extra workspaces at $19/mo each and extra seats at $8/mo each. Unlimited accounts means the account count never becomes the reason to add anything.
