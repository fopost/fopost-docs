# Starter

**$19/mo**, or **$180/year**.

For one person publishing their own work: a creator, a freelancer, a developer with a side project.

## What you get

| | |
|:--|:--|
| Social accounts | 10 |
| Networks | Every one |
| Posts | Unlimited |
| AI credits | 150/mo |
| Media storage | 5 GB |
| Workspaces | 1 |
| Team members | 1 |
| Link in Bio pages | 1 |
| Analytics | Basic |
| Support | Email |

## Included

- Publishing to every supported network
- Unlimited posts, drafts, and scheduled items
- Content calendar and bulk scheduling
- AI content rewriting and auto captions
- Carousels and video scheduling
- The [Owl Agent](/guide/using-fopost/owl-agent)

## Not included

- **Brand voices.** Available from Pro
- **Blog and URL to social.** Available from Pro
- **API access.** Available from Pro, or as a $5/mo add-on
- **Advanced analytics.** Basic analytics covers engagement and follower counts; the deeper breakdowns start at Pro
- **Extra seats.** Starter is one person. Adding anyone means moving to Pro

## When to move up

Ten accounts is generous for one person and tight for anyone managing a client. The usual reasons to move to [Pro](./pro) are wanting a second pair of hands, a second workspace to keep a client separate, or API access.
