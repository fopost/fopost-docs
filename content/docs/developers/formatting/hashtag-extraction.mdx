# Hashtag Extraction

A `Post` carries `tags`, which are plain words. `HashtagExtractor` turns them into hashtags each network will accept.

```php
use Fopost\Core\Formatting\HashtagExtractor;

$extractor = new HashtagExtractor();

$extractor->extract(['PHP', 'social media'], maxCount: 5);
// '#PHP #socialmedia'
```

Spaces and punctuation are removed, because `#social media` is two hashtags on every network and one of them is `#social`. Case is left alone: `#SocialMedia` is more readable than `#socialmedia`, and networks match case-insensitively anyway.

## In a formatter

Formatters call it during `format()`, so setting `tags` on the post is all you do:

```php
$post = new Post(
    title: 'Release notes',
    body: 'Version 2.0 is out.',
    tags: ['release', 'php', 'open source'],
);
```

Hashtags are appended within whatever character budget is left after the body and the URL. If they will not fit, they are dropped rather than truncating the content, since half a hashtag helps nobody.

## How many

Conventions differ sharply. Instagram tolerates many, LinkedIn expects a handful, X reads more than two or three as spam. Formatters cap per network, and `default_hashtag_count` in `FopostConfig` sets your own ceiling.

Order matters when they get cut: put the ones you care about first.

## Writing them by hand

If you want exact control, put the hashtags in the body and leave `tags` empty. Nothing is appended if there is nothing to append.
