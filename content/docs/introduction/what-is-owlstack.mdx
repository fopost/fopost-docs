# What is OwlStack?

**OwlStack** publishes, schedules, and manages content across 30 social networks. One dashboard, one API, one calendar.

## The problem

Managing more than two networks is mostly friction:

- Every network has its own format, limit, and quirks
- Posting the same thing everywhere means logging into five dashboards and retyping it four times
- Scheduling, analytics, and approvals live in separate tools, or nowhere
- Building even one network's API integration takes a week, and it breaks when they change it

## What OwlStack does

**Write once, publish natively.** One draft, a native version per network, a live preview of each. Not the same text pasted five times.

**Schedule and forget.** Posts go out from our servers. A calendar you can drag, bulk import, and repeat rules.

**Automate.** Feeds, webhooks, and recycling that resurfaces your evergreen winners with fresh variants.

**Measure.** Engagement, followers, top posts, demographics, best times, and campaign roll-ups.

**Collaborate.** Workspaces with real isolation, roles, and an approval step.

**Build on it.** The same REST API the dashboard uses, plus SDKs, an MCP server, and webhooks.

## Who it is for

**Creators**, publishing their own work to several networks and wanting the calendar and the AI to do the retyping.

**Teams**, needing approvals before something goes public and one place to see what is scheduled.

**Agencies**, running many clients where a post landing on the wrong account is a serious incident. A workspace per client is a hard boundary, not a filter.

**Developers**, integrating publishing into their own product without maintaining thirty API integrations.

## The two products

They share a name and are not the same thing.

| | Cloud | PHP library |
|:--|:--|:--|
| What it is | The dashboard and the [API](/api/overview) | An MIT package, [`owlstack/owlstack-core`](/developers) |
| Publishing runs on | Our servers | Yours |
| Credentials | Connected once via OAuth | Yours, per network |
| Networks | 30 | 11 |
| Scheduling, analytics, teams | Included | You build it |
| Account | Required | Not required |
| Cost | A plan | Free |

Most people want the Cloud. The library is for someone already in PHP who wants to own the whole pipeline and is happy registering apps with each network.

## AI

AI runs through the [Owl Agent](/guide/using-owlstack/owl-agent) and the composer: captions, per-network rewriting, hashtags, blog to social, voice notes, and image and video generation.

Two rules it follows. Nothing publishes without an explicit approval. And you pick an outcome, never a model.

## What it is not

Not an analytics suite, not a social listening tool, not a CRM, and not an ad manager. It publishes, schedules, and reports on what it publishes.
