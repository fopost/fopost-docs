# Why OwlStack?

## Thirty networks, on every plan

Not six, and not "more on the higher tier". Every plan reaches every network, including the writing platforms most tools ignore: Medium, DEV.to, Hashnode, Substack, WordPress.

[The full list](/introduction/platforms).

## Native per network, not one post copied

One draft becomes a version shaped for each network, with a live preview of each and overrides on the same screen. The alternative, which most tools ship, is posting the same 280 characters to LinkedIn and hoping.

## A real API, not an enterprise upsell

The [REST API](/api/overview) is the same one the dashboard runs on, available from [Pro](/guide/plans/pro) or as a $5/mo add-on. Official [TypeScript](/sdks/typescript) and [Python](/sdks/python) clients, an [MCP server](/sdks/mcp) so an AI assistant can drive it, and [webhooks](/api/webhooks) for every publishing event.

Scoped API keys, and a key can be bound to a single workspace so it physically cannot reach another.

## Workspace isolation that is actually enforced

Every request is checked against the workspace it names. A workspace you do not belong to is refused, from the dashboard, an API key, or the agent alike. There is no privileged path that sees everything.

For an agency, that is the difference between a careful process and a guarantee.

## AI with a person in the loop

The [agent](/guide/using-owlstack/owl-agent) drafts campaigns from your real material, in your [brand voice](/guide/using-owlstack/brand-voices), native per network. Nothing it produces publishes without an approval, which is a rule rather than a setting.

You pick a quality tier, never a model.

## Open source where it counts

The [PHP library](/developers), the [TypeScript SDK](/sdks/typescript), the [Python SDK](/sdks/python), the [MCP server](/sdks/mcp), and the [browser extension](/sdks/browser-extension) are all MIT and on GitHub.

The PHP library needs no account at all: it publishes to eleven networks from your own server with your own credentials, free.

## Honest about the networks that cannot be automated

Some networks have no write API. Tools that claim to post to them anyway are driving a headless browser against the network's terms, and the account that pays for that is yours.

OwlStack stages the post and a [browser extension](/sdks/browser-extension) publishes it from your own session, in one click. Slower, and it does not get anyone banned.

## What we do not claim

- **No free plan.** Every plan is a trial then a subscription
- **No self-hosting of the Cloud.** The PHP library runs on your infrastructure; the Cloud is ours
- **Team members are not unlimited.** One, three, or ten seats by plan, with extras at $8/mo
- **Analytics is publishing analytics.** It reports on your accounts and posts. It is not a listening or ad platform
